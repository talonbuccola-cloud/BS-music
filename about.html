About

BolognaSavage

Real words. Real identity. Real life lived.

Bologna Savage is an independent artist and songwriter whose work comes from lived experience, personal expression, and a refusal to create for the sake of fitting in.

His music blends raw emotion, sharp lyricism, dark humor, confrontation, reflection, and whatever else the song demands. What connects them is honesty.

The tattoos, the art, the style, and the attitude all come from the same place. This is more than music. It is a way of life.

Human-written.
AI-assisted. Artist-directed.

Bologna Savage writes the lyrics, develops the concepts, and directs the creative identity of his music.

Some releases use AI-assisted tools to create the instrumental production, shape the arrangement, and transform vocals into a voice that better fits the song.

That voice may sound male, female, older, younger, or completely different from Bologna Savage's natural singing voice. The decision is artistic. Each voice is chosen for the mood, character, and perspective of the track.

The technology may change the sound, but it does not write the story.

The lyrics, themes, concepts, and creative direction come from Bologna Savage.

On songs where he records the original vocal performance, AI tools may then be used to transform that performance into a different vocal identity. On other releases, synthetic vocals may be used as part of the production.

The result is music that combines human writing and artistic judgment with new production technology.

▮

More than music

Art, tattoos, fashion, skating, and self-expression all shape the identity behind Bologna Savage.

Every release is a reflection of a bigger picture.

It is not just about the sound. It is about the message, the visuals, the energy, and the world around it.

♛

The story
behind the name

Bologna Savage is built around contrast.

The name is strange, funny, memorable, and impossible to take as a generic artist label.

That same contrast shows up throughout the music: seriousness next to absurdity, vulnerability next to aggression, and rough edges beside careful creative decisions.

Independent
by design

Bologna Savage releases music independently and continues to experiment with new sounds, new tools, and new ways of presenting a song.

There is no fixed formula. Every method is chosen according to what best serves the song.
